# Qualitative findings

Five overlapping configurations are reconstructed from the news texts. The quotation register contains 22 Vietnamese passages, English translations, speaker attribution and source locations from nine articles in the 917-record analytical corpus.

The evidence package was assembled retrospectively on 29 September 2026. Case selection was purposive. Translations received AI-assisted source checks; independent human sign-off is not recorded. These cases support interpretation, not estimates of imaginary prevalence.

### I1 Educational augmentation

AI is assigned a supportive role in teaching or learning, made valuable through a stated educational benefit, with a relation to the learner’s or teacher’s continuing work.

**Include:** Require a source-level link between an AI function and educational practice or purpose. Assistance may change teaching roles and may coexist with dependency concerns.

**Do not infer:** A generic positive statement about AI, specialist workforce production, or a tool mention alone does not establish this configuration.

**Supporting passages:** E01;E02;E03;E12;E20. **Contrasting or qualifying passages:** E14;E16;E22.

E02 retains teachers while changing their role. E16 permits reference use while rejecting substitution for thinking. E22 confines the product to teachers. Augmentation therefore does not mean unrestricted student use.

**Overlaps:** I4 when policy, verification or instructional design conditions the benefit; I5 when support is distinguished from substitution; I2 only where the text makes capability formation explicit.

### I2 Readiness and literacy

Education prepares people to navigate AI-related social or occupational change by developing knowledge, skills, critical judgment or adaptability.

**Include:** Require a capability to be formed and a reason connected to living, learning or working with AI. Distinguish learning to use or judge AI from merely receiving its output.

**Do not infer:** A tool improving an existing task without a capability-development prescription is insufficient. Specialist AI production belongs to I3 unless a general readiness relationship is also explicit.

**Supporting passages:** E04;E05;E10;E13;E20. **Contrasting or qualifying passages:** E06;E07;E15.

E05 gives students an articulated concern, qualifying a learner-silence claim. E06 and E07 retain tension between replacement and performance-enhancement claims by the same business speaker. E15 questions whether tool fluency can coexist with weakened thinking.

**Overlaps:** I3 in the AIC programme, where broad youth preparation also nurtures specialist talent; I4 through critical and responsible use; I5 through threatened human capabilities.

### I3 Talent and innovation capacity

Education produces specialist AI personnel, research capacity or university–industry infrastructure linked to a projected institutional, regional or national technological future.

**Include:** Require specialist capacity formation and its purpose. National-development interpretation additionally requires an explicit collective national connection.

**Do not infer:** General AI literacy is not specialist capacity by itself. Do not infer national strategy from a Vietnamese institution, an AI degree title, or the code talent alone.

**Supporting passages:** E08;E09;E10;E11. **Contrasting or qualifying passages:** E17.

VNE19_001 supplies a narrower market-oriented programme announcement: specialist training is visible, but its relevant passage does not articulate the national-development claim found in E08 and E11. DTR19_002 is labelled business/product information, so its promised benefits remain promotional claims.

**Overlaps:** I2 where youth skills and specialist pathways coexist in LDG21_000. The two pre-ChatGPT programme reports establish historical examples, not prevalence or a national consensus.

### I4 Governed transformation

A changed educational practice or relationship becomes legitimate or workable through explicit rules, pedagogical design, verification, guidance or responsibility.

**Include:** Require a relation between educational use/change and a condition or prescription. A governance code and transformation code co-occurring does not suffice without a textual relationship.

**Do not infer:** Do not treat every risk mention as a rule, every ban as endorsement of adoption, or every call for support as a transformation claim. Retain the narrower governed-use label where transformation is not articulated.

**Supporting passages:** E03;E12;E13;E20;E21. **Contrasting or qualifying passages:** E18;E19;E22.

E13 favors instruction over prohibition. E18 restricts access on specified school infrastructure; E19 supplies redesign by another speaker. E21 sanctions undisclosed use while the article permits AI. These prescriptions differ in scope and cannot be merged into a universal governed-adoption position.

**Overlaps:** I1 when safeguards enable assistance; I2 where verification is a learned competence; I5 where integrity and retained human work justify boundaries.

### I5 Harm integrity and human limits

A threatened educational or human capacity, compromised integrity, or an explicit limit on AI is linked to a future worth avoiding or a role people must retain.

**Include:** Distinguish harmful-effect claims, integrity concerns and non-substitutability. Record the response: restriction, reference-only use, guidance or redesign.

**Do not infer:** A retained human role alone is not proof of harm. A risk claim is not necessarily a rejection of AI. A reported concern is not an independently demonstrated effect.

**Supporting passages:** E14;E15;E16;E18;E19. **Contrasting or qualifying passages:** E02;E07;E13;E20;E21.

E16 allows AI as reference; E13 opposes a ban; E18 supplies an actual scoped restriction. E02 retains the teacher without predicting harm. The configuration contains counter-imaginaries of loss and qualifications of otherwise favorable futures.

**Overlaps:** I1 through assistance versus substitution; I2 through the preservation of thinking and employability; I4 through integrity rules and restrictions.
