# SIPC codebook v2.3

Frozen v2.3 definitions used for completed full-source coding. Final checkpoint: 14 October 2026; 1,081 retained original article IDs after 17 researcher-error storage exclusions.

## Scope of revision

Only `future_role` changes. The other eleven field inventories are unchanged. The six families classify source-stated ends or outcomes, while literal roles, mechanisms and specific outcomes remain in each account. Positive and negative futures use the same family; desirability and claim direction retain their differences. These categories are empirical, not categories supplied by SIPC.

## Future role: six families

### FR_HUMAN_DEVELOPMENT — Human development and wellbeing

AI changes learners’ or educators’ intellectual development, agency, emotional or physical wellbeing, or supportive educational relationships.

**Include:** Explicit development or erosion of understanding, judgment, creativity or independence; explicit care, emotional support, protection from harm or their loss. Preserve the specific end and whether it is sought, expected or feared.

**Exclude:** Skills serving only employment or national development; resource savings; access distribution; legitimate educational judgments. Bare privacy, data theft or cybersecurity concerns without an explicit developmental, relational or wellbeing outcome are not automatically included.

**Boundary:** Code the focal end: human development/wellbeing here; occupational viability under FR_WORK_LIVELIHOODS; educational resources under FR_EFFECTIVE_PROVISION; participation distribution under FR_EQUITABLE_OPPORTUNITY; validity/fairness of institutional judgment under FR_TRUSTWORTHY_JUDGMENT. Care and intellectual development are distinct literal outcomes within one family, not duplicate codes.

### FR_WORK_LIVELIHOODS — Work preparation and occupational displacement

AI-related education prepares people for work, or AI changes the viability of educational/professional livelihoods through adaptation, replacement or displacement.

**Include:** Explicit employment preparation, professional adaptation, skill obsolescence or displacement, whether desired or warned against. Do not infer a deliberate displacement goal from a forecast job loss.

**Exclude:** AI knowledge with no work-related end; institutional cost savings alone; inferred job readiness from a technical degree.

**Boundary:** Individual/occupational work prospects are this goal. Explicit national standing is FR_COLLECTIVE_ADVANCEMENT. Teacher displacement can threaten livelihoods even when no replacement objective is endorsed.

### FR_EFFECTIVE_PROVISION — Educational productivity and resource priorities

AI reorganises the use of time, labour or money in educational provision, including workload relief and productivity claims or priorities shaped by cost reduction and profit.

**Include:** An explicit resource/productivity end or expected resource reordering. Preserve who gains and which priorities the source endorses or criticises; efficiency is not automatically desirable.

**Exclude:** Any institutional application; labour substitution without a stated provision/resource outcome; public purpose inferred from organisational scale.

**Boundary:** Ask whether the focal outcome is allocation/use of educational resources. Explicit learner or teacher time savings belong here; occupational displacement belongs to FR_WORK_LIVELIHOODS. Mere institution-wide deployment or monitoring supplies no resource end.

### FR_EQUITABLE_OPPORTUNITY — Educational inclusion and exclusion

AI changes who can obtain educational opportunities and whether existing educational inequalities narrow or widen.

**Include:** Inclusion, removing participation barriers, narrowing or widening educational inequality.

**Exclude:** Personalised instruction with no access/distribution end; free products with no inclusion claim; fair grading.

**Boundary:** The outcome concerns who can obtain educational participation/opportunities, rather than how well existing participants learn. Fair educational adjudication belongs to FR_TRUSTWORTHY_JUDGMENT.

### FR_TRUSTWORTHY_JUDGMENT — Trustworthy educational judgment and recognition

AI changes the authenticity of educational achievements and qualifications or the validity and fairness of institutional judgments about learners’ work or conduct.

**Include:** Authentic demonstration of learning, academic honesty, trustworthy qualifications, fair recognition, or explicitly accurate/fair institutional appraisal of conduct; warnings of false recognition, misjudgment or compromised assessment.

**Exclude:** Marking, monitoring or detection as functions without an explicit validity, authenticity or fairness end; generic factual accuracy; technical accuracy benchmarks; coder-inferred legitimacy.

**Boundary:** Assessment/monitoring is a mechanism. Assign this code only when authentic recognition or valid/fair educational appraisal is explicit. Accurate conduct appraisal can qualify; surveillance alone cannot. This classification records a claimed end and does not endorse the source’s surveillance practices.

### FR_COLLECTIVE_ADVANCEMENT — Collective development and education’s public purposes

AI-related education serves national technological/development ambitions or transforms education’s collective civic, cultural and public mission, including warnings of mission loss.

**Include:** National development, technological autonomy, competitive standing, cultural continuity, civic contribution or education’s public mission. Public/social order qualifies only when explicitly stated as a collective end connected to education; compliance tracking alone does not establish it.

**Exclude:** Vietnam as location; government as speaker; individual employability; generic social benefit inferred from education.

**Boundary:** Require an explicitly collective end. Developing specialists to improve national autonomy is here when autonomy is the focal end. Preparing a graduate for employment is FR_WORK_LIVELIHOODS. Preserve the specific collective goal literally rather than treating national competition and democratic life as interchangeable.

## Surveillance and uncovered ends

Removing a standalone control code does not remove surveillance futures. Preserve surveillance, discipline, loss of privacy and control relationships literally. Explicit valid conduct appraisal can receive FR_TRUSTWORTHY_JUDGMENT; explicit personal agency/wellbeing or collective public purposes can receive the respective family. Monitoring alone establishes none of those ends. Uncovered ends remain explicit in the account, with MISSING and a boundary note. Report missing assignments as “No assigned substantive code”, not “No future role”.

## Transition

A private v2.2 snapshot preserves prior records. Active records undergo source-based semantic role review. Do not mechanically rename codes. Existing extraction and the other eleven fields are retained unless a specific error is found. No revised full-corpus results are available yet.

## Complete twelve-field inventory

### future_role

What role is AI projected to play, and what educational or societal end or outcome is sought, expected or feared?

- `FR_HUMAN_DEVELOPMENT` — Human development and wellbeing: AI changes learners’ or educators’ intellectual development, agency, emotional or physical wellbeing, or supportive educational relationships.
- `FR_WORK_LIVELIHOODS` — Work preparation and occupational displacement: AI-related education prepares people for work, or AI changes the viability of educational/professional livelihoods through adaptation, replacement or displacement.
- `FR_EFFECTIVE_PROVISION` — Educational productivity and resource priorities: AI reorganises the use of time, labour or money in educational provision, including workload relief and productivity claims or priorities shaped by cost reduction and profit.
- `FR_EQUITABLE_OPPORTUNITY` — Educational inclusion and exclusion: AI changes who can obtain educational opportunities and whether existing educational inequalities narrow or widen.
- `FR_TRUSTWORTHY_JUDGMENT` — Trustworthy educational judgment and recognition: AI changes the authenticity of educational achievements and qualifications or the validity and fairness of institutional judgments about learners’ work or conduct.
- `FR_COLLECTIVE_ADVANCEMENT` — Collective development and education’s public purposes: AI-related education serves national technological/development ambitions or transforms education’s collective civic, cultural and public mission, including warnings of mission loss.

### certainty

How firmly does the speaker claim that the specified future will or can occur?

- `CERT_CONFIDENT` — Confident prediction: The speaker presents the future outcome as a firm expectation.
- `CERT_POSSIBLE` — Possible outcome: The speaker presents an outcome or capability as possible, probable or potential without assuring its occurrence.
- `CERT_DENIED` — Denied outcome or capability: The speaker explicitly rules out a specified future outcome or capability.
- `CERT_UNRESOLVED` — Explicitly unresolved outcome: The speaker explicitly withholds a prediction or states that an outcome cannot yet be determined.

### desirability

How does this speaker evaluate this particular future arrangement?

- `DES_POSITIVE` — Desirable: The speaker positively values the specified future.
- `DES_NEGATIVE` — Undesirable: The speaker negatively values the specified future.
- `DES_MIXED` — Explicitly mixed: One speaker explicitly weighs desirable and undesirable effects of the same future arrangement.
- `DES_UNDECIDED` — Value explicitly undecided: The speaker explicitly leaves the educational desirability of the future open.

### rationale

What explicit reason, need or value explains why this goal or arrangement is desirable or undesirable?

- `RATION_PEDAGOGY` — Learning quality: A learning mechanism or pedagogical principle warrants the future.
- `RATION_CAPACITY` — Operational feasibility and efficiency: Educational provision is justified or constrained by staffing, time, infrastructure, cost or implementation capacity.
- `RATION_ECONOMY` — Employment and economic returns: Expected employment, occupational demand, income or commercial returns warrant the future.
- `RATION_EQUITY` — Equality of opportunity: Fair distribution of educational opportunity warrants the future.
- `RATION_RIGHTS_INTEGRITY` — Rights, reliability and academic integrity: Protecting rights, trustworthy information, authentic achievement or fair adjudication warrants the future.
- `RATION_HUMAN_VALUES` — Human, civic and cultural values: Human flourishing, dignity, empathy, democratic life, civic responsibility or cultural expression warrants the future.
- `RATION_NATIONAL` — National development and strategic autonomy: The future is warranted by an explicit collective national-development, competitive-position or sovereignty objective.
- `RATION_POLICY` — Policy or standards compliance: Alignment with a named policy, formal standard or official requirement itself warrants the future.

### technology_object

What technical form of AI is explicitly implicated in the future?

- `OBJ_GENERATIVE` — Generative AI: AI is explicitly identified as a generative model/system producing new text, images, audio, video, code or other content.
- `OBJ_PREDICTIVE` — Predictive or discriminative AI: AI is explicitly identified as a non-generative system that classifies, scores, predicts, detects or recognises patterns in supplied data.
- `OBJ_UNSPECIFIED` — AI form unspecified: The source identifies AI but provides insufficient information to distinguish its model/system family.

### education_context

In what educational sector or setting is the future located?

- `CTX_SCHOOL` — School education: The future is located in early-childhood, primary or secondary education.
- `CTX_HIGHER` — Higher education: The future is located in university or other explicitly tertiary education and its academic institutions.
- `CTX_VOCATIONAL` — Vocational education: The future is located in an explicitly vocational education or training programme/institution.
- `CTX_PROFESSIONAL` — Professional and workplace development: The future is located in continuing preparation or development of people acting in occupational roles.
- `CTX_COMMUNITY` — Community and informal education: The future is located in non-occupational community education or explicitly informal self-education.

### timespan

How does the source characterise the proximity or continuity of the educational future, and what exact horizon does it supply?

- `TIME_IMMEDIATE` — Immediate / being realised: The future arrangement is explicitly being implemented or realised at the source’s reference time.
- `TIME_NEAR` — Near-term: The source explicitly characterises the future as near, soon or short-term.
- `TIME_DISTANT` — Distant / long-term: The source explicitly characterises the future as distant or long-term.
- `TIME_ONGOING` — Ongoing / continuous: The future is framed as a continuing or recurrent educational process rather than a target completed at a horizon.
- `TIME_ANCHORED_UNCLASSIFIED` — Explicit horizon; proximity unstated: A date, quantified interval or stage anchors the future, but the source does not characterise its proximity or continuity.

### space

Which country or cross-border arena is explicitly the intended reach of the educational future?

- `SPACE_VIETNAM` — Vietnam: The future explicitly applies within Vietnam.
- `SPACE_FOREIGN_DOMESTIC` — Specific foreign country: The future explicitly applies within a named country other than Vietnam.
- `SPACE_TRANSNATIONAL` — Cross-national or global: The future explicitly spans countries or is intended for a regional/worldwide educational arena.

### speaker

In what stated capacity does this actor articulate the future?

- `SPK_PUBLIC` — Public authority: The actor speaks in a governmental, legislative, administrative or security capacity.
- `SPK_ED_LEADERSHIP` — Educational institution or leadership: The actor speaks for an educational institution, programme or its leadership.
- `SPK_ACADEMIC_EXPERT` — Educator, researcher or specialist: The actor articulates the future as an educational practitioner, researcher or specialist adviser.
- `SPK_BUSINESS` — Commercial actor: The actor speaks for a company, provider or employer.
- `SPK_LEARNER` — Learner: The actor speaks as a person undertaking education.
- `SPK_FAMILY` — Family or caregiver: The actor speaks as a parent, family member or caregiver.
- `SPK_COLLECTIVE` — Civic or international organisation: The actor speaks for a civic, professional, community or intergovernmental organisation outside the preceding capacities.
- `SPK_AUTHOR` — Article’s authorial voice: The article advances a future claim in its own identifiable narrative or editorial voice.

### affected_entities

Who or what is explicitly expected to experience the consequences?

- `AFFECT_LEARNER` — Learners: People affected in their capacity as learners.
- `AFFECT_ED_WORKER` — Education and research workers: People whose educational or academic work is affected.
- `AFFECT_ED_ORG` — Educational organisations: An educational organisation or organised educational system is explicitly affected.
- `AFFECT_FAMILY` — Families and caregivers: Families’ educational decisions, care or resources are affected.
- `AFFECT_EMPLOYER` — Employers and non-education occupational actors: Employers, providers or occupational communities outside educational work experience consequences.
- `AFFECT_PUBLIC` — Wider communities or society: A community, society, nation or shared collective life is explicitly affected beyond the named learner/workplace entities.

### action

What concrete intervention or restraint is proposed to pursue or prevent the future?

- `ACT_CURRICULUM` — Change curriculum content or credentials: Actors change what is formally taught, required or recognised in a programme.
- `ACT_PRACTICE` — Change learning, teaching or assessment practice: Actors change how educational activities or learner evaluation are conducted.
- `ACT_RESOURCES` — Provide resources or implementation capacity: Actors obtain or supply the people, training, funding, equipment or services required for implementation.
- `ACT_RULES` — Set rules, safeguards or restrictions: Actors establish/enforce permissions, obligations, protections or prohibitions.
- `ACT_COORDINATE` — Establish coordination or partnerships: Actors create a specified joint arrangement for implementing or governing the future.
- `ACT_EVALUATE` — Evaluate the AI intervention: Actors produce or use evidence to decide whether an AI-related educational intervention should proceed or change.

### responsibility

Which actor is explicitly assigned the proposed intervention or accountability?

- `RESP_PUBLIC` — Public authorities: A public authority is assigned an intervention or accountability.
- `RESP_ED_ORG` — Educational organisations: An educational institution, its leadership or an organisational unit is assigned an intervention.
- `RESP_ED_WORKER` — Education and research workers: Teachers, instructors, researchers or educational staff are assigned an intervention in their professional capacity.
- `RESP_LEARNER` — Learners: Learners are assigned educational choices, conduct or accountability.
- `RESP_FAMILY` — Families and caregivers: Parents/families are assigned support, supervision or decisions.
- `RESP_BUSINESS` — Commercial actors: A company, provider or employer is assigned an intervention or accountability.
- `RESP_COLLECTIVE` — Civic or international collectives: A community, civic/professional/intergovernmental organisation or explicitly identified wider collective is assigned an intervention.
